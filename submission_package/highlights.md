# Highlights (Acta Astronautica; <=85 chars each)

- Terrestrial north is encoded as relations to a network of celestial sources
- Reconstruction is stress-tested over 10^3–10^8 yr of source loss and drift
- Source-class stability, not heterogeneity, dominates at long horizons
- RANSAC-like decoding tolerates misidentified sources where least squares fails
- Information cost and reliability form a Pareto frontier for system design
