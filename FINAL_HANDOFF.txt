HEADLINE RESULTS (final revision)
----------------
- Quasar (ICRF-like) n=50: P(err<1 deg) = 1.0 at 1e7 yr; 0.0403 at 1e8.
- Hybrid M7 (best tested heterogeneous): 0.9711 at 1e7 yr; 0.0138 at
  1e8 -- i.e. the homogeneous quasar network OUTPERFORMED the tested
  hybrid. H2 (heterogeneity advantage) is NOT supported under the
  baseline scenario; long-horizon composition is governed by assumed
  class stability/usability/geometry, not diversity per se.
- Single landmark: a single source carrying only the source-north
  relational scalar is insufficient for unique 3-D reconstruction
  (p_invalid = 1.0) -- a property of the relational encoding. The M1b
  direct-axis control (direction defines the axis) is algebraically
  valid while the landmark survives but is capped near 0.5 by the
  unresolved mirror ambiguity (no handedness channel) and collapses
  under drift/loss (0.027 at 1e5 yr; invalid 0.9645 at 1e6; catalogue
  substitution corrupts unbuffered: 0.239 at 1e4, p_mismatch=0.5).
- Star-only networks collapse by ~1e6 yr (drift).
- RANSAC-like: P<1deg = 0.91 at p_mismatch = 0.5 (LS ~0.004).
- Chirality triples: removes mirror failure entirely (wrong-mirror
  fraction 0.0 vs 0.49), P<1deg ~0.49 -> 0.97 at 1e7 yr.
- Negative controls NC1/NC2 fail as required (P<1deg ~0).
- Stored-edge identity check: helps substitution (0.8135->0.835), neutral
  under injection (1.0->1.0), impossible under permutation (E6b/E6c).
- Allocation frontier (E7): best baseline allocation at 1e7 yr is
  quasar 20 only (P=1.0); quasars/galaxies dominate at long horizons.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS (declared in manuscript)
------------------------------------------
- Usability/drift parameters are scenario assumptions (Weibull, drift
  rates, 0.5 mirror prior), not fitted; sensitivity grids supplied
  (E3, E4, Figures 5-7).
- Synthetic populations; catalogue section is descriptive.
- Mirror ambiguity modeled as O(3) reflection; chirality subset test.
- M1b has no handedness channel: its ~0.5 success cap is the expected
  mirror coin-flip, not estimator noise.
